# Account Deletion Policy

_Last updated: August 5, 2026_

## Requesting Account Deletion

If you would like to permanently delete your account and all associated data, please send an email to our support team with the following information:

- **Full Name**
- **Registered Email Address**
- **Phone Number** (registered with your account)

This information is required to verify your identity and prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

## Deletion Process

Once your email is received, your account deletion request will be processed immediately. Your account and associated data will be permanently deleted as soon as your request is verified.

**Please note:** Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account has been deleted, it cannot be recovered.

## Important Notice

- **Do not expect a reply** to your account deletion email.
- The absence of a response does **not** mean your request has been ignored.
- Your account will be deleted automatically after your deletion request is received and verified.
